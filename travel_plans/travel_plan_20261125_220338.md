## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Berlin**
- **Morning:** Fly into **Berlin Brandenburg (BER)** – most major airlines serve this hub.  
- **Transfer:** Private shuttle or airport‑train (RE7) to city centre (≈30 min).  
- **Check‑in:** **Hotel Indigo Berlin** (stylish boutique, central location, vegan‑friendly breakfast).  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Walk the historic **Brandenburg Gate** and **Pariser Platz**.  
  - Guided visit to the **Reichstag Dome** (pre‑book tickets for panoramic city views).  
- **Evening:** Vegan dinner at **Kopps** (modern German‑Nordic cuisine).  
- **Overnight:** Hotel Indigo Berlin.

---

### **Day 2 – Berlin Deep‑Dive**
- **Morning:**  
  - **Museum Island** – pick two museums (Pergamon & Neues Museum are top picks).  
  - Coffee break at **Vux** (vegan pastries & specialty coffee).  
- **Mid‑day:** Stroll along the **East Side Gallery** (the longest remaining Berlin Wall stretch).  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Picnic in **Tiergarten** (grab a vegan snack from **Veganz**).  
  - Optional: Bike tour of the city’s “green” neighborhoods (Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg).  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Lucky Leek**, a Michelin‑starred vegan restaurant.  
- **Overnight:** Hotel Indigo Berlin.

---

### **Day 3 – Dresden (Day‑Trip or Overnight)**
- **Travel:** Early ICE train Berlin → Dresden (≈2 h, 2nd class €35, 1st class €55).  
- **Morning:** Arrival, store luggage at the hotel, then walk to **Frauenkirche** (reconstruction story).  
- **Mid‑day:** Explore the **Zwinger Palace** and its art collections.  
- **Lunch:** Vegan‑friendly **Café KUNST** (salads, soups, and house‑made seitan).  
- **Afternoon:** River Elbe promenade & optional **Pfunds Molkerei** (the most beautiful dairy shop in the world – just for the Instagram shot!).  
- **Evening:** Return to Berlin or stay overnight: **Hotel Bülow Palais** (luxury boutique, central Altstadt).  
- **Overnight:** Hotel Bülow Palais (if you choose to stay in Dresden) – otherwise back to Berlin.

---

### **Day 4 – Munich – Bavarian Heartbeat**
- **Travel:** ICE Berlin → Munich (≈4 h).  
- **Check‑in:** **Bayerischer Hof** (historic luxury, central, excellent vegan breakfast buffet).  
- **Morning:** Guided walk of **Marienplatz**, watch the Glockenspiel show (11 am).  
- **Lunch:** **Tian** (vegetarian/vegan haute cuisine) in the city centre.  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit **Viktualienmarkt** – sample vegan pretzels, fresh fruit, and local brews (non‑alcoholic options).  
  - Relax in the **English Garden**; stop at the **Chinese Tower beer garden** (they serve a vegan “Bavarian” sausage).  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Max Pett**, a fully vegan restaurant with a modern twist.  
- **Overnight:** Bayerischer Hof.

---

### **Day 5 – Fairy‑Tale Castles & Alpine Lakes**
- **Excursion:** Full‑day guided tour to **Neuschwanstein Castle** & **Füssen** (pick‑up at 8 am, return 7 pm).  
  - **Neuschwanstein** – early entry ticket (pre‑book) to avoid crowds.  
  - **Hohenschwangau** (the king’s childhood home) optional.  
  - **Lake Alpsee** – short hike around the lake (great photo ops).  
- **Lunch:** Pack a vegan picnic (sandwiches, fruit, nuts) or dine at **Schlossbrauerei** (vegan menu available).  
- **Evening:** Return to Munich. Free time to explore the nightlife or a relaxed walk around the illuminated **Olympic Park**.  
- **Overnight:** Bayerischer Hof.

---

### **Day 6 – Hamburg – Port City Vibes**
- **Travel:** ICE Munich → Hamburg (≈6 h).  
- **Check‑in:** **The Westin Hamburg** (luxury, stunning Elbphilharmonie views) – or **Madison Hotel Hamburg** for a boutique feel.  
- **Morning:** Guided harbor cruise (30 min) to see the massive container terminals and historic **Speicherstadt** (warehouse district).  
- **Lunch:** Vegan lunch at **Happenpappen**, a casual spot near the harbor.  
- **Afternoon:**  
  - Visit **Miniatur Wunderland** (world’s largest model railway).  
  - Walk the **Elbphilharmonie Plaza** for panoramic city views (free, but timed entry).  
- **Evening:** Dinner at **Fischereihafen Restaurant** – they now offer a dedicated vegan menu with “fish‑free” specialties.  
- **Overnight:** The Westin Hamburg.

---

### **Day 7 – Return to Berlin & Departure**
- **Travel:** Early ICE Hamburg → Berlin (≈2 h).  
- **Morning:** Last‑minute shopping on **Kurfürstendamm** or a quick visit to **Charlottenburg Palace** (if time permits).  
- **Brunch:** Vegan brunch at **The Bowl** (smoothie bowls, tofu scrambles).  
- **Afternoon:** Transfer to **Berlin Brandenburg Airport** (airport‑train or private shuttle).  
- **Departure:** Fly home with memories (and a suitcase full of vegan snacks!).

## LOGISTICS & PRACTICAL TIPS  

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **International Flights** | Most travelers arrive via **Berlin Brandenburg (BER)** or **Munich (MUC)**. Choose the city that fits your airline route; the itinerary can start in either and end in Berlin for a smooth departure. |
| **Rail Pass** | German Rail (DB) offers a **7‑day German Rail Pass** (€260 2nd class, €380 1st class) – unlimited travel on ICE, IC, and regional trains. Great for flexibility. |
| **Local Transport** | City transport (U‑Bahn, S‑Bahn, trams, buses) is covered by a **Berlin WelcomeCard**, **Munich CityTourCard**, and **Hamburg Card** (all include discounts on attractions). |
| **Currency** | Euro (€). ATMs are plentiful; credit cards widely accepted, but keep a small cash reserve for markets. |
| **Language** | German; English is spoken in tourist areas. Learning a few basics (“Bitte”, “Danke”, “Entschuldigung”) is appreciated. |
| **Vegan Dining** | Germany’s vegan scene is booming. Use the **HappyCow** app to locate vegan/vegetarian spots on the go. |
| **Travel Insurance** | Recommended: coverage for medical, trip cancellation, and luggage. |
| **Time Zone** | Central European Time (CET, UTC+1) – summer months observe CEST (UTC+2). |
| **Weather (May‑June)** | Pleasant 15‑25 °C (59‑77 °F). Pack layers, a light rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes. |
